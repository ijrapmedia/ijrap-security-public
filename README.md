## Why IJRAP Security Exists

Across the world, investigative journalists, human rights defenders, whistleblowers, and field auditors face two existential threats:
1. **The Crisis of Digital Manipulation:** Deepfakes, edited media, and altered metadata make it increasingly difficult to prove that field evidence is authentic and unedited.
2. **Hostile Field Conditions & Censorship:** Investigators frequently operate in zero-internet zones where devices risk physical seizure, forced unlocking, or remote tampering.

**IJRAP Security** was engineered to solve both problems at the root. Built on the **IJRAP (Investigative Journalism Rap)** methodology—which unites grassroots cultural expression with rigorous forensic science—this engine transforms any standard mobile or desktop device into a tamper-proof field evidence vault.

## Core Technical Capabilities & Impact

* **4-Pillar Synchronized Evidence Sealing:** Simultaneously binds **Live Audio**, **Live Video**, **Live Photo**, and **Structured Written Testimony** (with WGS84 GPS coordinates and UTC timestamps) into a single indivisible cryptographic block.
* **Hybrid Post-Quantum Cryptography:** Protects long-term evidentiary value by combining classical **SHA-256** with quantum-resistant **SHA-3-256 (Keccak-256)** hashing to generate an unalterable master seal.
* **OS-Level Write-Once-Read-Many (WORM) Immutability:** Immediately locks captured media files and cryptographic manifests in strict read-only mode (`0o444`) upon capture, preventing silent deletion or post-capture alteration.
* **Offline-First Resilience:** Operates with zero reliance on external commercial cloud providers, ensuring full autonomy in disconnected or high-risk environments.

## Real-World Social & Institutional Impact

* **Protecting Press Freedom & Whistleblowers:** Empowers independent reporters and citizens to document corruption, environmental harm, or public health anomalies with mathematically verifiable proof.
* **Strengthening Institutional Accountability:** Provides NGOs, legal observers, and public-interest watchdogs with an open, auditable standard for chain-of-custody verification.
* **Dual-Tier Ecosystem Architecture:** This core sealing engine forms the foundation of the **IJRAP Citizen Public Tier** (planned for release under the **GNU AGPLv3** open-source license upon grant execution), complementing the closed-source **IJRAP Enterprise Institutional Platform** tailored for sovereign governments, banks, and international agencies.
